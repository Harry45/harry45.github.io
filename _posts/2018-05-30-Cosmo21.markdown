<p align="justify">The <a href="http://ada.cosmostat.org/">ADAIX</a> summer school and the <a href="https://cosmo21.cosmostat.org/">COSMO21</a> conference are typically held together, and took place in Valencia from 20 to 25 May 2018. The ADAIX summer school is practical in focus, with the goal of familiarising researchers with the latest tools and techniques in data science, which will be invaluable in the era of massive surveys. The topics covered included:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Machine Learning</li>
  <li>Bayesian Methods</li>
  <li>Sparsity-based analysis</li>
  <li>Analysis of astronomical data with Python</li>
  <li>Compressed sensing based instrumentation</li> 
</ul>

<p align="justify">COSMO21, by contrast, consisted of a series of research talks. Those I found particularly interesting included:</p>
